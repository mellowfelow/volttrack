# VoltTrack — Backlink & Off-Site Authority Plan

Backlinks are links from **other** websites to volttrackhub.com. They cannot be
created in code — they're earned off-site. This is the plan to earn them, ordered
by effort-to-payoff. The site itself is already link-worthy (schema, content, a
free tool at `/electric-dirt-bike-street-legal-by-state/`); this is the outreach.

Why zero right now: the site is new and the domain (volttrackhub.com) is only days
old. Every item below is a lever to change that.

---

## Tier 1 — Free, self-serve, do this week (fastest wins)

These are backlinks you can place yourself by creating/claiming a profile.

- [ ] **Google Business Profile** — google.com/business (also drives local + Maps)
- [ ] **Bing Places** — bingplaces.com
- [ ] **Manufacturer dealer locators** — highest-value, most-relevant links you can get.
      As an authorized dealer, email each and ask to be added to their "Where to Buy":
      - Sur-Ron, Talaria, STACYC, KTM, Stark Future (your authorized brands)
- [ ] **Social profiles** (each is a backlink + needed for schema `sameAs`):
      Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, X, LinkedIn. Use the exact same
      name/URL/logo on all. → send me the URLs and I'll add them to Organization schema.
- [ ] **Business directories**: Chamber of Commerce, Yelp, Yellow Pages, Trustpilot,
      Better Business Bureau, Apple Business Connect.

## Tier 2 — Niche relevance (where your buyers are)

Relevance beats volume. One link from an e-moto community outranks 50 generic ones.

- [ ] **Reddit**: r/Surron, r/ebikes, r/electricvehicles, r/dirtbikes — be a genuine
      participant; link your free checker / guides only when they answer a question.
- [ ] **Owner forums**: Sur-Ron forums, endless-sphere.com, electricbike-forum.
- [ ] **Facebook groups**: Sur-Ron/Talaria/Stark owner groups.
- [ ] **EV & powersports blogs/news**: Electrek, Electric Bike Report, RideApart —
      pitch your data/tool (see Tier 3).

## Tier 3 — Digital PR / linkable assets (biggest long-term payoff)

People link to *useful things*, not to store homepages. You now have one asset;
these earn the editorial links.

- [ ] **Promote the free tool**: `/electric-dirt-bike-street-legal-by-state/` —
      pitch it to the blogs/forums above as "a free street-legal checker."
- [ ] **Original data/roundups**: "Fastest electric dirt bikes 2026", price guides,
      "cheapest Sur-Ron" — publish, then outreach to anyone covering the topic.
- [ ] **HARO / Connectively / Featured**: answer journalist queries on EVs/off-road
      as an expert dealer → earns news-site backlinks.
- [ ] **Guest posts** on powersports/EV blogs.

## Tier 4 — Partners & suppliers

- [ ] Battery/parts brands you resell (Chi Battery Systems, eWatt, EBMX) — ask for a
      "stocked by / retailer" link.
- [ ] Financing partner, shipping partner, any local dealers/clubs.
